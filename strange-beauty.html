Strange Beauty

Fall/Winter 2021

Inspired by abandoned buildings in Georgia, this collection examines the beauty found in wear, decay, and deterioration over time. The designs reference peeling paint, exposed wires, cracked walls, and forgotten interiors, translating these details into bold lines, geometric shapes, and distressed construction. Hanging sleeves, cuts, safety pins, and chains reflect structures that are barely holding together, emphasizing impermanence and fragility. Despite the decay, the collection highlights the strange beauty and resilience of what remains standing.
