Renaissance Romance

Spring/Summer 2020

The Renaissance marked a cultural rebirth that celebrated beauty, humanism, and artistic expression, shaping fashion as a symbol of identity, femininity, and romantic idealism. Defined by sculptural silhouettes, voluminous sleeves, corsetry, and refined detailing, dress became an extension of craftsmanship and self expression. This collection draws from those historical forms, using materials like lace, brocade, and cotton sateen to evoke a sense of luxury. Traditional elements are refined with clean cuts and contemporary construction, resulting in silhouettes that feel both timeless and relevant.
